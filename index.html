Principles, built in

How the app carries what Brevy stands for.

  • Only what needs you. The feed holds a handful of cards; Brevy carries the rest.
  • A person, one tap away. Ask for a human anywhere; the request is always honored.
  • Receipts on every card. Facts show their source and a verified date.
  • Edit anything Buzz writes. Summaries and care plans are yours to correct.
  • Nothing without consent. Recording and sharing are opt-in, visible, revocable.
  • Meets you where you are. The same help arrives by text or phone; the app is never required.
