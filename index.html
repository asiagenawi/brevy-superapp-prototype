Priority tiers

P0 · Critical / time-bound — risks coverage or safety. Pinned to top; may push-notify.
P1 · Needs you soon — appointments, med doses, items awaiting your input.
P2 · In progress / FYI — status updates and partner notes; no action required.
P3 · Suggested — benefits, providers, resources; dismissible, at most one at a time.

Why this feed looks like this

Rules that keep it calm

Slot budget: about 3 cards above the fold; one hero, the rest condensed.
Rollup: same-type items collapse into one stack — tap "In motion."
Suppression: dismiss a suggestion and it cools down; snooze reschedules.
Channel discipline: only P0/P1 ever push — watch for the notification, or tap the bell.
